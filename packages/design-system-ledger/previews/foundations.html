Colour

Surfaces--lg-canvas · --lg-surface · --lg-well

Two surfaces and a well, rather than a four-step ramp. The well is deliberately darker than the canvas in both themes: cards sit above the page and hold chrome, wells are cut into it and hold evidence. Keeping those opposed is what makes "this is data the system actually read" legible.

--lg-canvas the page
--lg-surface cards, panels, bars
--lg-well evidence, digests, commit lists
--lg-hover row and control hover
Accent--lg-accent · --lg-accent-solid

Ink blue, and interactive affordances only: actions, links, focus, active nav. It sits outside the verdict family so it can never be mistaken for a result. In light mode both tokens hold the same value, because this blue clears 7.6:1 as text on paper and as a fill under white text. Only dark mode has to split them.

--lg-accent text, icons, links
--lg-accent-solid filled backgrounds
--lg-accent-subtle selected row, info alert
--lg-accent-rule hairline on tinted surfaces
Verdict family--lg-satisfied · --lg-unsatisfied · --lg-danger · --lg-neutral

Four meanings that must never share a hue, plus an attention colour that is not a verdict and an archived tone that is orthogonal to all of them. The negative verdict is rendered in ink rather than given a colour: it cannot then sit near the red reserved for things that actually broke, it survives greyscale, and it is honest about weight.

--lg-satisfied verified, satisfied
--lg-unsatisfied eval_failed, not_satisfied
--lg-danger rate limit, crash, blown ceiling
--lg-neutral new, never evaluated
--lg-warning approaching a limit, never a result
--lg-archived archived, orthogonal to status
Text tones--lg-text-*

Secondary clears 6.6:1 and muted clears 4.6:1, so both carry information. Faint is under 3:1 by design and is for non-essential content only: disabled labels and decorative counters, never a fact the reader needs.

Primary. Independent machine attestation over real source at pinned commits.

Secondary. The record's integrity is separable from the judgment's correctness.

Muted. Help text, captions, table headers, metadata.

Faint. Disabled labels only.

Type

ScaleGeist · --lg-text-50 … --lg-text-800

Numeric, so "is 400 bigger than 300" is never a question, and in rem, so a reader who raises their browser's base font size gets a system that grows with them. 300 is the default UI size. 400 is the reading size and appears only where there are real sentences, meaning evaluator rationales and requirement descriptions.

Micro-label.lg-micro-label

Uppercase, tracked, 11px, and wayfinding only: the label above a field group, a column group or a definition term. It is not a decorative eyebrow above a section heading. A heading that needs a label above it to be understood needs a better heading, and there is at most one of these per surface.

Space, shape and rules

Space scale--lg-space-1 … --lg-space-8

A 4px scale, in rem. 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64.

4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64
Shape--lg-radius · --lg-radius-pill

Square. The rule is that surfaces and controls have no radius and only chips and avatars are round, so there is no middle radius to get wrong and no decision to make per component. It is also what makes a shared-edge button group a one-line negative margin.

surface chip
Rules--lg-rule · --lg-rule-strong · --lg-rule-ink

Three weights, three jobs. The ink rule is this direction's signature and appears only under a page or section heading. It is typographic and carries no state: no surface in this system gets a coloured edge or side tab to encode status, because an accent band down one side of a card is the most recognisable machine-generated tell in this class of interface.

--lg-rule
--lg-rule-strong
--lg-rule-ink
Density context[data-density="compact"]

Density is a context attribute rather than a pair of fixed row tokens. The comfortable values are the default and the compact context re-points the same tokens, so a commit table can be dense inside a page that is not, and no component needs to know which mode it is in. The switch in the header changes every specimen on this page.

Motion--lg-dur-1/2/3 · --lg-dur-indicator

Interaction durations drop to zero under prefers-reduced-motion. The indicator duration is separate and only slows, because freezing a spinner or an indeterminate bar makes a request that is genuinely still running look hung, which misinforms rather than accommodates. Purely decorative motion, meaning the skeleton shimmer, stops outright.

--lg-dur-1
80ms, tone and colour on hover
--lg-dur-2
140ms, entering and leaving elements
--lg-dur-3
200ms, larger layout reveals
--lg-dur-indicator
1100ms, and 2600ms under reduced motion. Never zero.
